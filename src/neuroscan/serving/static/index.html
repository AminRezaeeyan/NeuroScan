    What happens to your image

    1. 1

      Input checks

      The file must decode as an image of sensible size, and be grayscale like an MRI. Color photos stop here.

    2. 2

      Out-of-distribution test

      The image's features are compared with the training MRIs. Too far from all of them, and the model refuses to guess.

    3. 3

      Calibrated classifier

      EfficientNetB0 with temperature scaling, so "90%" means right about 9 times in 10. Low-confidence cases go to review.

    4. 4

      Grad-CAM

      A heatmap of the regions that drove the decision. A sanity check, not an explanation a clinician can rely on.

    Trained on the Brain Tumor MRI Dataset (Masoud Nickparvar, CC BY 4.0), evaluated on a duplicate-aware split. Image-level evaluation on one public dataset: performance on other scanners or hospitals is unknown.
